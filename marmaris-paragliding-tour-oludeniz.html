Marmaris to Ölüdeniz Paragliding Tour

The ultimate day trip from Marmaris. We handle everything — hotel pickup, scenic coastal drive, Babadağ tandem flight, and free time at the Blue Lagoon.

Marmaris → Ölüdeniz Paragliding Day Trip: Full Itinerary

  1. 06:30 — Hotel Pickup: Air-conditioned minibus collects you from your Marmaris hotel (İçmeler, Turgutreis, or Marmaris center).
  2. 06:30–09:00 — Scenic Coastal Drive: The route follows the stunning D400 highway along the Turquoise Coast, passing through Dalaman and Göcek with sea views the entire way.
  3. 09:00–09:30 — Arrive Ölüdeniz: Safety briefing and equipment fitting at our Fethiye/Ölüdeniz base camp.
  4. 09:30–10:30 — Drive to Summit + Takeoff: Cable car to Babadağ Mountain launch zone (1,960m). Your THK-licensed pilot prepares for takeoff.
  5. 10:30–11:15 — Tandem Flight (25-45 min): Soar over the Blue Lagoon, Butterfly Valley, and the entire Lycian Coast. Eagles fly beside you.
  6. 11:15–14:00 — Free Time at Blue Lagoon: Lunch at a beachfront restaurant, swim in the turquoise water, explore Ölüdeniz village.
  7. 14:00–16:30 — Return to Marmaris: Relax on the drive back. You'll be at your hotel by 16:30.
