Is Paragliding Safe in Ölüdeniz?

The honest answer: yes, with the right operator. Here's exactly how we keep you safe — the same information we'd give a government auditor.

The Short Answer

Tandem paragliding in Ölüdeniz has an outstanding safety record. Over 100,000 tandem flights launch from Babadağ Mountain every year. The Turkish Aeronautical Association (THK) supervises all operations with regulations stricter than most countries.

However, safety depends entirely on who you fly with. An unlicensed pilot with old equipment is dangerous anywhere. A licensed, insured operator at a regulated site is statistically safer than a long car ride.

Has Anyone Died Paragliding in Ölüdeniz?

This is the question everyone Googles but is afraid to ask. The honest answer: fatal incidents at Babadağ are extremely rare and almost exclusively involve solo pilots (not tandem passengers) making errors in challenging conditions. Commercial tandem operations — where you fly with a licensed professional — have an exceptional safety record.

The key factors: pilot licensing, equipment age, insurance coverage, and weather discipline. Read our detailed safety infrastructure page for the full technical breakdown.

Red Flags: How to Spot an Unsafe Operator

  • 🚩 Price under $120: They're cutting corners — mountain fee, insurance, or pilot quality.
  • 🚩 Can't show pilot license: Ask to see the THK T2 card. No card = don't fly.
  • 🚩 No insurance document: Demand to see the aviation policy. Ours is always available.
  • 🚩 Flies in bad weather: A reliable operator cancels and refunds. A reckless one flies anyway.
  • 🚩 Old, faded wing fabric: Wings should be replaced every 300 hours. UV damage = weaker fabric.
