What Is Ölüdeniz Paragliding?

Ölüdeniz paragliding is a tandem (two-person) flight where you and a professional pilot launch from Babadağ Mountain — one of the highest coastal takeoff points in the world at 1,960 meters above sea level — and glide down to Belcekiz Beach on the shores of the famous Blue Lagoon in Fethiye, Muğla, Turkey.

The flight typically lasts 25 to 45 minutes depending on thermal conditions, wind speed, and the time of day. Morning flights (before 10:00) offer the smoothest, calmest air — perfect for nervous first-timers. Midday flights (12:00–14:00) provide the strongest thermals, meaning pilots can climb even higher than takeoff altitude, sometimes reaching 2,500+ meters. Evening flights reward you with the legendary Ölüdeniz sunset — arguably the most photographed sunset paragliding view on earth.

No experience is necessary. In a tandem paragliding flight, the pilot handles 100% of the controls — takeoff, steering, and landing. Your only job is to run 3–5 steps during launch and enjoy the view. That's why tandem paragliding in Fethiye is one of the top adventure activities for tourists visiting the Turkish Riviera. Over 100,000 tandem flights are completed safely from Babadağ every year.

Your Ölüdeniz Paragliding Experience: Step by Step

From hotel pickup to landing on Belcekiz Beach — here's exactly what happens during your Fethiye tandem flight.

1

Hotel Pickup — Fethiye, Hisarönü, or Ölüdeniz

Our air-conditioned minibus collects you from your hotel or pension. Pickup is 60 minutes before your scheduled flight time. Covered areas include Fethiye city center, Çalış Beach, Ovacık, Hisarönü, and all Ölüdeniz hotels. The drive to Babadağ Mountain takes approximately 40 minutes via the serpentine mountain road, rising from sea level to 1,960m.

2

Safety Briefing at the Babadağ Launch Site

At the takeoff area, your THK T2-licensed pilot introduces themselves, checks the live wind data from the anemometer station, and walks you through the 30-second takeoff procedure: "When I say run, you run straight ahead — do NOT sit down." The harness and helmet are fitted, and lines are checked by both the pilot and a ground safety officer.

3

Takeoff — 1,960m Above the Blue Lagoon

You take 3–5 running steps and the EN 926-certified tandem wing inflates above you. Within seconds, the ground drops away and Ölüdeniz's Blue Lagoon unfolds below — a crescent of turquoise water surrounded by pine-covered mountains. The first 30 seconds are the most thrilling. After that, it's pure peacefulness.

4

25–45 Minutes Soaring Over Fethiye's Coastline

Your pilot navigates thermals (columns of warm rising air) to extend the flight. On strong thermal days, you may climb above the peak itself. Below, you'll see the Butterfly Valley, St. Nicholas Island, Kayaköy ghost village, and the entire Fethiye Gulf. If you chose the 4K Media Bundle, your pilot captures everything on a GoPro HERO 12.

5

Landing on Belcekiz Beach, Ölüdeniz

The flight ends with a smooth landing on the designated paragliding strip at Belcekiz Beach. Our ground team secures the landing zone. Your 4K video and 100+ photos are transferred to your phone instantly via Wi-Fi. The entire experience — from hotel pickup to beach landing — takes approximately 2 to 2.5 hours.

Why Ölüdeniz Is the World's Paragliding Capital

Ölüdeniz, located in Fethiye, Muğla Province, Turkey, has been the global epicenter of tandem paragliding since the early 1990s. The reason is pure geography: Babadağ Mountain (Mount Baba) rises 1,969 meters directly from the Mediterranean Sea within just 5 kilometers horizontally. This extreme verticality creates reliable thermal uplift and a massive altitude window for extended flights.

The Ölüdeniz Blue Lagoon — a UNESCO-protected natural reserve — sits at the base of Babadağ, creating what pilots call a "visual runway." You're flying directly toward one of the most beautiful natural landscapes in the Mediterranean. The lagoon's warm water generates consistent onshore breezes (the "Imbat" wind) that help extend flight times during summer months.

The Ölüdeniz Air Games Festival — one of the largest international paragliding competitions — is held here annually, attracting 100+ pilots from 30+ countries. The Turkish Aeronautical Association (THK) and the Fédération Aéronautique Internationale (FAI) officially recognize Ölüdeniz as a Class-A paragliding destination.
