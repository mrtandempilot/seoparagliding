What You Need to Book

  • Preferred date & time: Morning (08:00) or afternoon (14:00) pickup. We recommended morning for the smoothest air.
  • Hotel name or location: We pick you up from anywhere in Fethiye, Hisarönü, Ovacık, or Ölüdeniz.
  • Number of passengers: Group discounts available for 3+ people. See our prices.
  • Package choice: Professional Tandem ($150) or 4K Media Bundle ($190).
  • Weight: Max 100kg for men, 85kg for women. See FAQ for more.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your flight. If we cancel due to weather, you get a full refund or free reschedule — your choice.

We assess Babadağ weather conditions at 06:00 on your flight day. If wind speeds exceed safe limits, we'll notify you immediately via WhatsApp.
