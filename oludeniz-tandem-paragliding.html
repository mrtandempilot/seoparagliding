What Is Tandem Paragliding?

Tandem paragliding is when you fly attached to a professional pilot using a specially designed two-person wing. Your pilot controls everything — takeoff, steering, thermals, and landing. You simply enjoy the ride. It's the safest way to experience paragliding because you benefit from your pilot's 1,500+ hours of flight experience.

In Ölüdeniz, tandem flights launch from Babadağ Mountain at 1,960m — one of the highest commercial launches in the world. The result? 25-45 minute flights with extraordinary views.
